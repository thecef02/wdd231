Non Profit Membership

Cost: Free

  • Basic business directory listing
  • Access to community networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter
  • Certificate of membership

Bronze Membership

Cost: $50 / year

  • Everything in NP level
  • Enhanced directory listing with logo
  • 1 chamber event discount per year
  • Mention in the monthly newsletter
  • Ribbon cutting ceremony for new businesses

Silver Membership

Cost: $150 / year

  • Everything in Bronze level
  • 3 chamber event discounts per year
  • Access to exclusive business training sessions
  • Advertising banner on the chamber website
  • Social media feature (1 per quarter)
  • Priority in chamber referral network

Gold Membership

Cost: $300 / year

  • Everything in Silver level
  • Spotlight position on the home page
  • Unlimited chamber event discounts
  • Premium advertising placement on website
  • Monthly social media feature
  • All training sessions free of charge
  • Dedicated chamber account manager
  • VIP seating at annual chamber banquet
